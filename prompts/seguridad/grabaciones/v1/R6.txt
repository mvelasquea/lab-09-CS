**Acceptance criteria – Cancel an appointment**

- **Given** a patient with a confirmed appointment, **when** they press "Cancel" and confirm, **then** the appointment is shown as cancelled and the slot is released.
- **Given** an appointment that has already been attended, **when** the patient opens it, **then** the "Cancel" option is not available.
- **Given** a cancelled appointment, **when** the patient opens it, **then** they see its status as cancelled.
